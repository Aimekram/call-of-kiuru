Call of Kiuru: Meadow, early and late

Meadow, with three ways to colour early and late. The data is real: Cologne's modelled start of spring 1940–2026 and the 2026 ranking of all eight cities. Hover the bars.

Red against blue reads as "too hot" against "fine", so the pink is gone. Both colour pairs below pass the colourblind and contrast checks in light and dark. Lilac against blue was tried and failed: for red-green colourblind readers the two look alike.

1 · Sun and sky

Early is ochre, late is blue: a warm spring against a cold one, which is what drives the model. Warm, but not the red of a warning.

2 · Lilac and ochre

Early is lilac, after the blossom; late is ochre. Neither colour carries a meaning of good or bad, but late has no obvious reason to be ochre.

3 · Ink only

No colour for direction at all: above the line is later, below is earlier, and the numbers carry the sign. Nothing to misread, but slower to scan.
